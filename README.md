## 🚀 Featured Project

### 🛍️ E-Commerce Customer Behaviour Analysis

**A Data-Driven Marketing and Customer Retention Framework**

An analytics project focused on understanding online customer behaviour and developing data-driven marketing strategies.

**Project objectives:**
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns and product performance.
- Explore customer behaviour through exploratory data analysis (EDA).
- Identify customer segments using behavioural and purchasing patterns.
- Investigate customers who may be at risk of becoming inactive.
- Develop recommendations to improve customer engagement and retention.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn · Jupyter/Google Colab
